Luna turns any course material into quizzes, problem sets and flashcards, then tracks every answer to find why each student struggles, and tells students, parents and teachers exactly what to do next.

The problem
1

No one knows why a student struggles.A 70% hides whether it is a topic gap, a missing core skill like algebra, or carelessness.

2

Material never becomes practice.Notes and past exams sit in files; making good practice takes hours.

3

Generic AI forgets.Chatbots generate content, but do not track results or decide the next step.

How it works

A closed loop: material in, next step out

1UploadNotes, books, past exams, handwriting, formulas, charts
2GenerateBuilt-in or custom AI agents: quiz, problem set, flashcards, summary
3PracticeAnswer inside Luna, or export PDF / Word / slides
4MeasureEvery attempt scored by topic, skill and type of mistake
5Next stepLuna's read: what to fix, already in plan vs. new to create
↺ Results feed the next activity and update the study plan
